# Proposing a correction

Please check the original scan when suggesting a reading. AI suggestions and repeated guesses are not independent evidence.

Use a correction issue, or complete the blank proposal, reviewer/date and evidence columns in the unresolved-readings register. Include:

1. The edition name and version, followed by the source-page/block locator, such as `05-025-P02`.
2. The existing wording and your proposed reading.
3. A scan citation and explanation of the visible letter forms or other evidence. Describe uncertainty explicitly.
4. Your name or preferred attribution and the review date.

Keep literal transcription, a plain-English rendering, and genealogical interpretation distinct. A later identifying slip may disagree with the manuscript and should be recorded as a separate source of information.

Corrections are proposals until reviewed. The maintainer should update the structured transcript and corresponding text, HTML, Word and PDF exports together, regenerate the uncertainty register and download package, and document the change. Avoid changing just one export and leaving conflicting copies.

Maintain the existing source and paragraph locators where possible. Record any unavoidable locator change in the change log. Do not overwrite an earlier version tag.

Do not upload credentials, private correspondence, or new personal information about living people to a correction issue. Access to this private repository is limited to people the owner chooses to invite.
