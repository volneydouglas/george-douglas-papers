# Review of unresolved readings, September 2026

Scope: all 1,200 items of `editions/annotated/Supporting-files/Unresolved-readings.tsv` (version 1.0 of the annotated transcription).

**How this review was made, and its limit.** The six source PDFs are not stored in this repository, and this review session could not obtain them: ACU Digital Commons refused scripted downloads (Cloudflare challenge), a headless-browser download and an Internet Archive lookup were blocked by the session's policy, and no copy was found in the owner's Google Drive or Gmail. **No scan image was consulted.** Every decision below rests on (1) the surrounding text, (2) the same name, place or formula read confidently elsewhere in the papers, (3) duplicate scans of the same leaf, (4) calendar or legal-form checks, and (5) the independently prepared plain-English family edition in `editions/family/Plain-text/`, which was made from the same scans by a separate reader and serves here only as a second witness, never as proof.

**Consequence.** Because a wrong confident reading is worse than a preserved marker, **no marker was removed and no transcript text was changed** in `Transcripts.json`, `Complete-transcript.txt`, `Complete-transcript.html` or the PDF/Word exports. All findings are recorded (a) in this log and (b) in the register's `proposed_correction`, `reviewer_and_date` and `evidence` columns, which the repository's `CONTRIBUTING.md` designates for exactly this purpose. Items marked "recommended for acceptance on image check" are the ones a reader with the scans should look at first.
