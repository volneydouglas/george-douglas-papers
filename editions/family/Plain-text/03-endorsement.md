## Pages 2-2
### Cover label

Endorsement of the congregation for Sylvana Church. George Douglas. September 1888. Elders H. Douglas and H. [G.?] Orr.

## Pages 3-3
### The congregation approves his preaching

This certifies that Brother George Douglas, a member of the Church of Christ worshiping at Sylvana, Ellis County, Texas, has for some time been working in preaching and teaching. Wishing him to continue this work, we are pleased to state that he goes with our full permission and wholehearted approval.

May he share the Apostle Paul's conviction, “Woe to me if I do not preach the Gospel of Christ,” always remembering the solemn charge to Timothy to preach the word, and so forth.
