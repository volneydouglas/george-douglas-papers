GEORGE DOUGLAS PAPERS — REVISION 1.1 REVIEW SUMMARY
20 September 2026

All 1,200 original uncertainty items on 193 scan pages were processed by the
local image/OCR workflow (1,351 runs, zero errors). This was not a fresh
individual visual reading of all items. There are 81 focused item decisions,
including the earlier 50-item pilot; 1,119 items remain machine-processed only
in this round. No reading was accepted from OCR agreement alone.

Applied: 28 resolved readings, two partial surname recoveries, 16 clearer
witness references and six additional changes to surrounding text. Eleven
family-text corrections and seven editorial-note synchronizations accompany
the revision. Three new doubts were added. The current annotated register
contains 1,175 uncertainties. A marker can represent several words and is not
an error count. Stable original item IDs are retained; retired IDs are not reused.

All 387 supplied scan pages remain covered. Both editions are AI-assisted and
have not received independent human verification or ACU endorsement. The
initial family reading was separate; this revision uses comparisons and is
not a blinded independent reading. Further image and human review may help.

The annotated package's Supporting-files includes the full item ledger,
combined decisions, change records and export-validation summary. Detailed
OCR, source-image evidence and historical baselines are retained in the
private repository under review/full-run-2026-09. Ledger review_record paths
refer to that repository's review directory; the combined Review-decisions.json
is supplied for readers using the package without the repository.
