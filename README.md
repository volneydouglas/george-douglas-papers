# George Douglas Papers

An independent family project prepared for Volney Douglas from scans supplied by Abilene Christian University Special Collections and Archives. The collection is the George Douglas Papers, 1830–1939, Center for Restoration Studies MS 5.

**Public review edition · 26 September 2026 · version 2.0**

Both editions now account for all **932 scan pages in ten source PDFs**. This update adds **545 pages** from Folder 4 correspondence, N. L. Douglas’s daybook, Folder 6 correspondence, and the poems and other writings in Folder 7. Covers, envelopes, duplicates and fragments are included. Complete coverage does not mean every word has been recovered: **3,415 unresolved-reading entries** remain explicitly marked.

## Preparation and review

These editions were prepared with AI assistance. Every new scan page received an initial visual reading, with selected enlargement, image adjustment and comparison with related passages in the collection. Local Apple Vision OCR at four orientations served as a reading aid. The other agent’s transcription was excluded from this addition. The new reference and family readings were prepared together from the scans and are not independent of each other. The earlier edition has its own disclosed comparison history.

All 584 pages of the new PDF exports were visually inspected, and their text was checked against Word and the structured master. These checks establish export consistency, not independent human verification of the handwriting. Difficult readings remain marked and can be improved. Nathaniel Lynn Douglas’s father and birthplace have not been established.

This is an independent family contribution, with thanks to ACU for preserving the collection and supplying scans. It is not an ACU publication or an endorsed scholarly edition. Historical assertions and language remain attributable to their writers.

The repository was made public on 20 September 2026 at Volney Douglas’s request. Its pages and downloads can be shared without a GitHub invitation. No separate public website is deployed. See [rights and publication status](RIGHTS.md) and [the change log](CHANGELOG.md).

The `v1.0-review` tag preserves the first published review snapshot. No university submission is made as part of this update; `Draft-cover-note.txt` remains an unsent draft.
